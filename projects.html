ASME Journal of Thermal Science and Engineering Applications · October 2023

Enhancing Gas Turbine Power Plant Performance in Tropical Climates by Using Inlet Air Cooling with Desiccant Dehumidification and Evaporative Cooling

This study investigated an inlet-air-cooling approach combining evaporative cooling, a desiccant wheel, and a Maisotsenko cooler. Aspen HYSYS and MATLAB models indicated that the system could reduce compressor inlet temperature by an average of 11.5 °C and improve peak-condition power output by more than 2 MW, with an estimated cooling-system power requirement of about 0.9 MW.

B.Sc. Mathematics thesis · Universidad del Norte · July–November 2022 · Cum Laude

Analytical Semigroups of Linear Operators and an Application to a Thermoelastic System

Applied the theory of strongly continuous semigroups of bounded linear operators to a thermoelastic bar problem, reformulated as an abstract Cauchy problem. The work established existence and uniqueness, continuous dependence on initial data, and analyticity of the solution independently of initial-data regularity.

Microfluidic Droplet Formation in Flow-Focusing Geometries

Studied droplet size and frequency under different flow rates in a two-phase flow-focusing device, and explored transient double emulsions using two sequential flow-focusing geometries. The work included fabrication of specialized microchannels using soft lithography.

TUM exchange program · Thermo-fluid dynamics · October 2023–September 2024

Computational Study of Potential Flow Around an Airfoil

Simulated incompressible potential flow around NACA 0010 and NACA 4424 airfoils using a finite-volume discretization. Recovered velocity fields, calculated pressure distributions and pressure coefficients along the airfoil surfaces, and compared the lift coefficient at zero angle of attack with reference results.
